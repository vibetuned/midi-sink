#### #1 — Step 83: the Toda lattice soliton ring, `voice_kind` 10 (2026-10-19; Voxo 0.23.0, additive; evidence `docs/evidence/step83`)

**What the spec asked** (`_work/ROADMAP_6.md` step 83; `TO_PROJECT_SPEC` §13.13): N nodes on a ring under the exponential potential, symplectic Verlet at ×4 (step 78 the prerequisite), the press and CC 74 the nonlinearity, poly pressure a momentum IMPULSE (the ring conserves Σp — gate delta b), near-integrable not integrable (gate delta a: the shape gate replaces "never breaks into chaotic noise"); the gates: the soliton's shape within 2 % over ten minutes at b_max, the lossless drift under 0.001 dB over ten minutes, no alias blow-up at full compression, the strobe portrait, the profile. **What shipped is §13.13's Toda paragraph as corrected.** The decisions:

1. **The state is double.** The first ring, in float, held the shape but drifted 0.009 dB a minute lossless — the rounding of `q += p` at 192 kHz, a random walk the gate's 0.001 dB over ten minutes cannot absorb. In double the minute-mean H drifts 0.000017 dB over the ten minutes; the ring is 16–64 nodes, the cost 1.1 % of a core a voice at A2 (3.0 % at 64 nodes). Nothing else in Suzu is double; the ring's smallness pays for it.
2. **The drift is the minute mean's.** The leapfrog conserves a modified H; the Toda H itself breathes ±0.06 dB at b_max (the soliton's local frequency times the step) with no trend. The gate reads the mean over each minute against minute 1 (`drift`), and prints the breathing beside it — the honest reading of "the lossless energy drift".
3. **THE RESOLUTION RULE, found by the red control.** A fortissimo C7 blew up under the stiffness bound (k·cap ≤ 0.9, the membrane's kind of bound): measured on the ring alone, the leapfrog makes energy once a step's displacement over the exponential's length, √k·ĉ·b, passes ~0.5 uncapped and ~0.22 with the cap engaged — whatever the cap's shape (hard or soft gave the same rows). So the ring's rate follows the pulse: two to eight inner steps a 2× sub-step (`toda_m`, the momenta rescaled exactly on a change, the pickup still read twice a sub-step at the ×4 rate), holding √k·ĉ·b under 0.15; and where eight cannot reach it, the nonlinearity is CAPPED FOR THE NOTE at the strike, with a 1.3× allowance for the self-focusing (the struck 0.76 becomes a 0.97 soliton). The first form of the limiter followed the live peak every block and PUMPED the pulse (the potential re-shaped under it; C7 at level 1 went +162 dB in a second under the rule) — a ceiling fixed at the strike, lowered only if the pulse outgrows the allowance, never raised. The inspection says what the rule did (k[6] m, k[7] b asked, k[8] the ceiling, k[1] b applied).
4. **The soft cap.** `min(e^{−br}, cap)` left a kink in the force the leapfrog fed on at the margin (m 4, A 3 at C7: +40 dB hard, +0.5 dB soft); the cap is now C¹ — E = e up to cap/2, cap − cap²/(4e) past it — with the potential's integral in `energy()`, and the stiffness bound stated for it (k·cap ≤ 0.9 → the knee's stiffness 0.45).
5. **The pickup stands in the lab against the mean drift, in lattice coordinates.** The spec's impulse is inaudible to a pickup fixed to a node (a Galilean boost leaves every bond's compression as it was). A fully Eulerian pickup (ξ + q(ξ) = x, Newton each step, the bonds interpolated) hears the boost but reads the density spike of a hard soliton — the lattice near-touching, 1 + r ≈ 0.04 — as −43 dB of top-octave content (linear) / −43 (cubic): the physics of a point probe, not an artifact, and not a sound to ship. The pickup's lattice coordinate now runs back at Σp/N a step (the mean drift alone) and reads a Catmull–Rom cubic over the bonds: the Doppler is the Galilean one (+19.7 % at full poly pressure, J 0.25 of the sound speed), the top octave stays at the half-bands' floor (−132 dB). A density pickup could be an option later.
6. **The level is the strike's strength.** `A = level · vel_gain` scales the strike's strain and the output divides it back, so a louder patch presses the ring harder (more soliton) — the Duffing's and the membrane's rule, kept; the book says so.
7. **The headroom gate (54): sixteen rings C3–C7 at full press, the bar 30 % (18.6 % measured).** The rule's inner steps make the top notes dearer (C7: m 4 at 16 nodes, m 6 at 64); the storm and the tablets' spikes below. **The tablets' tier caps the inner steps at four and the nodes at 32** (`SUZU_TODA_M_MAX` 4 and the clamp in `voxo_set_suzu_params` under `VOXO_MOBILE`, with a log line — the plate's kind of cap): the note's ceiling on b takes what four cannot — the top notes a little tamer there.
9. **The exponential is the ring's own.** With libm's `exp` the Tab's first spike read 756 XRuns and a render of 13.8 ms a burst (step 82's run: 0 and 1.9 ms) — a double exponential per node per inner step, and bionic's is slow. `suzu::toda_exp` (Cody–Waite range reduction, a degree-11 Taylor on |f| ≤ ln2/2, 10⁻¹⁴ relative, the 2^k through the exponent field) is plain arithmetic the compiler vectorizes across the nodes, the same bits on every platform (the force stays the exact gradient of a potential within 10⁻¹⁴ of Toda's; the ten-minute drift re-measured 0.000004 dB); the Mac's cost fell from 1.3 to 1.1 % a voice at A2 and from 18 to 9 % at C7 with 64 nodes; the Tab's figures below. `energy()` keeps libm's `exp` as the reference.
8. **Found at the step, in Voxo:** the inspection's H must be reported in one unit across inner-step changes (per-step units scale by m²) — k[2] is in the ×4 step's units; the probes' first drift figures compared different units. In the harness: `tools/make_patch_preset.cpp` (the step-82 generator, generalised: a patch table entry through the preset writer, the file gate 28 pins). No core change.

10. **The tablets.** The iPad: the spike on the Toda ring patch, 0 XRuns, the render at most 0.42 ms of the 2.67 ms burst. **The Tab: 731 XRuns and a render of up to 13.9 ms a 4 ms burst** — the same pattern as step 80's note (XRuns at the 4 ms burst with the step-80 APK) and step 82's unison pair, whose render there is 1.9 ms where the Mac's is 0.05 ms: the Tab's audio thread runs some forty times slower than the Mac's core on any voice (a little core at a low clock, by the ratio), and the ring at four to seven voices costs the Mac 0.2–0.3 ms a burst — over that budget by itself. **The bisect: the same signed release APK on the Pixel_10_Pro emulator, arm64 at the Mac's speed, reads 0 XRuns and 3.6 ms of a 20 ms burst** — the Android build of the engine is sound; the device's thread placement is the limit. **Carried, for the author (a shell item, not Voxo's):** the Tab's audio thread wants a performance hint — Android's ADPF (`APerformanceHint`, API 31) or an affinity to the big cores from the AAudio callback — before any of the heavier voices (the membrane, the ring) can be judged there; until then the Tab's spike on them is a record, not a gate.

**Evidence** (`docs/evidence/step83/SUMMARY.md`): the Release suite (229 green, gates 49–54), the Debug ctest (14/14), the web gate (−118 dB, 3.5 % of the quantum), the lab gate green, the storm (0 XRuns on the preset and on the voice, the render at most 0.63 ms), the profile, the strobe chart, the tablets and the emulator.

#### #2 — Step 84: the Boris–leapfrog magnetic gyroscope, `voice_kind` 11 (2026-10-21; Voxo 0.24.0, additive; evidence `docs/evidence/step84`)

**What the spec asked** (`_work/ROADMAP_6.md` step 84; `TO_PROJECT_SPEC` §13.13): a particle on a 2D potential surface under a perpendicular field, the half kick → the exact Cayley rotation → the half kick; (x₁, x₂) straight to (L, R); the spirograph into `trace/`; poly pressure → B; its own drive clock for the strobe; gate delta a (volume-preserving, the energy bounded, not exact) and b (the spring and B both prewarped); the gates: the doublet within 0.1 cent across B ∈ [0, 50] up to 2 kHz, the red 1.2 cents at 2 kHz charted, the energy's bounded oscillation over ten minutes, the spirograph verified in Kessho, the profile. **What shipped is §13.13's Boris paragraph as corrected.** The decisions:

1. **The Cayley rotation is three shears, not a cos/sin matrix.** A rotation matrix whose c² + s² misses 1 by a unit in the last place is a contraction or a gain of that amount applied every sub-step — e^{±1.7} over ten minutes at 96 kHz, a systematic drift no "bounded energy" gate survives. t = tan(θ/2), s = sin θ, t again is exactly [[cos θ, −sin θ], [sin θ, cos θ]] in exact arithmetic and a round-off walk in floating point, like every other shear in Suzu; the state is double besides (the ring's precedent). Measured: ±0.031 dB of breathing from the kick's splitting, the minute means drifting 0.000000 dB over ten minutes.
2. **The doublet is measured from the struct's own map, not from a formula the engine also holds.** The map on z = x₁ + i·x₂, w = y₁ + i·y₂ is complex-linear (the rotation rotates both components together, the kick and the drift are real scalings), so two basis steps give its 2×2 and the eigenvalues' arguments are the two lines; gate 55 does that against ω_B/2 ± sqrt(ω₀² + ω_B²/4) over five notes and six fields, then checks the engine's inspection reports the same lines, then gate 56 finds them in the rendered channel. The analysis (the angles θ/2 ± ψ with cos ψ = cos(θ/2)(1 − ε²/2); the residual (ω_B·dt/2)²/6) is in `suzu.h` and the book. The two prewarps alone meet the gate with three orders of margin — no Newton fit of (ε, θ) to the doublet was needed, and none was added.
3. **B is in hertz and the poly pressure's, with a rest value.** The roadmap's "B ∈ [0, 50]" read as a cyclotron frequency in Hz is the musical range (the doublet's split: a chorus at a few Hz, a tremolo-wide pair at fifty) and makes the 1.2-cent red at 2 kHz come out exactly as the roadmap says; `boris_b` is the field at full poly pressure and `boris_b_rest` the field without it (the patch 2 and 24 Hz). The proposal's "at B = 0 a mono planar string" is a patch with the rest field at 0; the default keeps a keyboard without poly pressure in stereo. The field is smoothed with the swirl and refitted every block, so a press sweeps the precession without a step.
4. **The strike is a displacement along the cell's position by the equal-power pan law** (√(1−u), √u): the pluck position is the pan — 0.5 the diagonal with both channels THE SAME BITS (the mono planar string, gated bit for bit), 0 the left alone, 1 the right. The cell's strike position from the play surfaces (`voxo_set_strike_pluck`) therefore pans this voice.
5. **The first stereo voice in `render_suzu_other`:** (x₁, x₂) to (L, R) with a second SVF for the right channel, the bus plate fed the mean of the two, the release's amplitude √(2H). The strobe's own clock (clock 2) is the cyclotron period — the section at each full turn of the field; with no field it falls back to the bass.
6. **The spirograph in Kessho is the author's viewing:** the bench's `--boris-demo <dir>` traces four notes under a poly-pressure sweep into Kessho and into Anod's spark reading (stills every half second, `docs/evidence/step84/spirograph/`). The trace's pair is (x₁, x₂) as drawn, the aux pair the energy and the field.
7. **Found at the step:** nothing in the core and nothing in Voxo's older voices. The harness: `tools/boris_chart.py` (the doublet's error over the keyboard, prewarped and raw) and the strobe chart's Boris panel; the bench's demo polls its last export out before the next pass (an export in flight refuses a new one — the Anod pass had produced no stills).
8. **The cost and the tablets.** The web gate reads the gyroscope 100 % bit-identical to the desktop reference (no libm in the step; the fit's tan and sin once a block) at 0.24 % of the quantum; the storm 0 XRuns with the render at most 0.13 ms; the iPad's spike 0 XRuns at 0.10 ms of the 2.67 ms burst; **the Tab was unplugged at the step** — the Pixel_10_Pro emulator stands in (`android-emulator-stands-in-for-tab`): 0 XRuns, 1.1 ms of a 20 ms burst. The profile is flat to a hundredth of a dB across the keyboard (a linear voice at a fixed strike).

**Evidence** (`docs/evidence/step84/SUMMARY.md`): the Release suite (239 green, gates 55–58, gate 28 on nineteen), the Debug ctest (14/14), the web gate, the lab gate, the storm, the profile, the doublet chart, the strobe chart, the spirograph stills in Kessho and Anod's spark reading, the iPad and the emulator.
