Marble mode. Tap or click = ink drop · drag = comb · two-finger twist = vortex · two-finger pinch = fold · pen = stylus wake (pressure sets the tip) · mouse: right-drag = vortex, Shift-drag = fold, middle-drag = wake. A MIDI instrument plays straight in on Chrome/Edge. Settings live in the panel at the top left (the desktop app's settings window, in the browser).
This browser has no WebGPU. midi-sink's marbling engine needs it (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26, Firefox 141+).
