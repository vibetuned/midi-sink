The spirograph

The particle's position in its plane — the left and right channels. With no field it is a line through the centre; the field turns the line into a precessing rosette, one circular mode turning with the field and one against.

The strobe

the pair at the cyclotron period — the voice's own clock

One dot per turn of the field: the rosette seen stroboscopically is the slower ellipse of the doublet's beat. The suite counts thirty-nine returns at twenty hertz.
