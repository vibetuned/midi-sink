Suzu lab / The Toda ring

An exponential lattice on a ring, stepped in double: softly struck it is a bell of linear modes on the note; pressed hard it launches solitons that cross and keep their shape — and the resolution rule that keeps the integrator lossless as they sharpen.

The ring

each node's bond compression, from the engine's positions

The nodes around the ring, each drawn out by its bond's strain r = q₍ᵢ₊₁₎ − qᵢ: a compression pulls the node in and lights it. A soft strike is a standing wave; a hard press under the strike is a pulse that runs round the ring — the soliton — and the pickup stands still against the ring's mean drift.

The strobe

the ring's pair at every bass period (the Poincaré section)

A linear ring returns to a few points; a soliton's returns walk a loop at the pulse's own period over the note's — the shape the suite counts (eighteen percent of the period for sixteen rings).
