Suzu lab / The membrane and the gong

A two-dimensional sheet inside a zero ring, stepped by the leapfrog: the drum, whose energy the step keeps exactly, and the gong — the sheet's stretching raises its stiffness, so a hard strike lands sharp and settles on the note.

The sheet

the displacement u, node by node, from the engine's state

Every interior node of the grid, drawn from the engine's own array: dark where the sheet is pulled down, light where it rises. The ring around it is held at zero — simply supported — and the grid is cut per note so the leapfrog's Courant number stays under the bar.

The strobe

the sheet's pair at every bass period (the Poincaré section)

One dot per return of the reference phasor at the note: a drum's modes are not harmonic, so the dots walk a ring rather than sit; the gong's swell bends the walk as the stretching decays.
