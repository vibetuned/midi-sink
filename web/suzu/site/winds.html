Lab mode — the red controls

Air through the valve depends on the pressure in the mouthpiece AFTER the air arrives, so the engine solves the valve and the bore together in each step (the implicit junction). The naive junction takes the pressure from the step before: the continuous system is passive, the discrete one is not, and a transient sets it growing. The engine probes every patch when it loads and refuses this one. Make the bore lossless so nothing hides the energy made, bypass the gate, then bend the note.
