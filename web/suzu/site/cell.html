The leapfrog keeps the orbit's size exactly. The naive update — x and y moved together from the same old values — multiplies the orbit's area by 1 + ε² every sub-step: at A3 that is twenty-thousandfold a second. The engine ships the leapfrog; this switch shows why. The lab mutes the sound and restarts the engine when it blows up.

The glide's retune: the shipped form re-bases the orbit as the pitch moves; the plain recurrence stepped once a block ripples the level — glide and watch the size.
