midi-sink is two MIDI devices in one. As a **visualizer** it accepts any
dialect — MPE, wind, classic — and decides which it is hearing. As an
**instrument** (Play mode on the tablets) it generates a standard MPE stream
that feeds its own visualizer through a loopback *and* leaves the device to
your DAW. Both directions are on this page.

The output sections below were **verified against real byte logs** — sessions
recorded at the shell's single merge point on the iPad and the Galaxy Tab,
one per source: fingers, stylus, control strip, session configuration.
`tools/chart_check.py` replays those logs against this very table and fails
if a documented message is missing, a forbidden one appears, or the log
contains anything the chart does not describe. The input sections are the
normalizer's contract, pinned by its headless golden suite.

## How the modes are chosen

The normalizer runs a light heuristic, overridable by the host
(`sumi_set_input_mode`):

* an MPE Configuration Message, or note-ons spread across channels 2+ with
  per-channel bend or pressure → **MPE mode**;
* notes only ever on one channel plus a dense CC 2 stream → **wind mode**;
* otherwise → **classic mode**.

Airwave-style CC streams are orthogonal — routed by the CC map in every mode.
On the tablets, entering Play mode pushes the MCM and RPN 0 = 48 into the
loopback *first*, so the visualizer's mode and bend range are deterministic,
never heuristic.

## Ownership — one consumer per dimension

| Dimension | Setting | 0 (default) | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-note pitch bend | `bend_mode` | glide — drags the drop along the pitch axis | ripple — shimmers the water, the drop holds |
| Channel pressure (0xD0) | `press_mode` | ink feed — unbounded boundary growth | Lamb–Oseen swirl |
| CC 74 (slide) | `slide_mode` | per-drop hue / ink density | Hamiltonian pinch at the drop |

Poly key pressure (0xA0) always drives the swirl; the master bend always
shears; switching a mode is clean in both directions (no stale-delta passes).

## Reading a DAW recording of a Play-surface performance

A recorded finger performance replays exactly: the centre bend before every
strike keeps attacks in tune, X-bends are semitone-scaled against the declared
±48 range, pressure and poly pressure re-drive the feed and the swirl, and the
Note Offs land after their pressure-0. A recorded **stylus** performance
replays every note, bend, CC 74 and pressure — and no wakes. The wake is
physical: it is the only mark on the water a DAW cannot make.
