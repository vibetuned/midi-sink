**Lineage.** The vortex is Lamb–Oseen's, as on [the swirl page](../swirl/);
the chord's flow is Kirchhoff's point-vortex system, whose Hamiltonian the
free mode integrates; the splitting is Strang's; the consonance is Sethares'
reading of Plomp and Levelt's roughness.

**What it does.** Under Kessho (or `bend_mode` 5 anywhere) a chord is a vortex
system. The lowest held note is the bass; every other voice's interval against
it, bend included, is read on a consonance curve $C \in [-1, +1]$ — Sethares'
sensory dissonance over the first six partials, anchored at the unison
($+1$), the tritone ($0$) and the minor second ($-1$); at C4 the fifth reads
$+0.68$, the fourth $+0.34$, the major third about zero, the octave $+1$.
The circulation is

$$
\Gamma_k = \operatorname{sign}(C_k)\,\Gamma_{\text{vel}}\,(0.3 + 0.7\,|C_k|),
$$

the bass taking $\Gamma_{\text{vel}}$ with the positive sign, the floor made
continuous through $C = 0$ so a bend through the tritone flips the sign
without a jump; the *Braid couple* control scales the others' circulations.
Same-sign pairs co-rotate and braid about a hyperbolic point between them;
opposite-sign pairs are a dipole and jet, carrying their ink.

**Never integrated.** The ink is advected by the superposed flow
$\psi = \sum_k \Gamma_k\,\psi_{LO}(x - x_k; r_c)$, but no flow is solved:
each frame the existing exact swirl passes — one per voice with the
half-circulation $\Gamma_k\,\Delta t / 2$ — are composed in **Strang
order**, $1 \ldots N$ then $N \ldots 1$. That is a second-order splitting of
the superposed flow in which every factor is a rotation, so the composition
has $\det J = 1$ exactly; the saddle and the cat's-eye separatrix emerge in
the composed field. Fifteen voices are thirty passes, within the budget of
sixty-four. The sequence at $-\Gamma$ is its exact inverse, which is what the
soak's pairs use.

**Pinned or free.** Pinned (the default), the vortices stay at their keys and
the ink braids and jets between them. Free, the vortices themselves move under
each other's flow: Kirchhoff's point vortices under
$H = -\tfrac{1}{4\pi}\sum_{j<k}\Gamma_j\Gamma_k\ln(\lVert x_j - x_k\rVert^2 + r_c^2)$,
stepped by the implicit midpoint rule — symplectic, keeping the centre of
vorticity and the angular impulse exactly and $H$ to a few parts in a billion
over ten minutes — so consonant notes orbit each other and dissonant ones
travel as a pair, carrying their ink. A note re-anchors at its key when
struck; a lifted note's vortex fades over a quarter second and goes on its
way; the sheet's edges hold the vortices five percent in. The two are the
A/B item B-1, both built, decided by viewing.

## Ownership

* **Per-note bend** under Kessho's default table (`bend_mode` 5; the *Braid
  consonance* choice in the settings): the drop holds, the bend moves the
  interval and so the circulation. The *Braid couple* control (CC) scales the
  non-bass circulations; *Braid vortices* in the settings picks pinned or
  free.
* **No gesture of its own:** `sumi_add_vortex` already composes.
