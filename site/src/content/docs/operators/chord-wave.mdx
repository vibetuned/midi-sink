**Lineage.** Jaffer's comb and v2's ripple: a shear along one coordinate by a
function of the other is exact for any profile, and the sine ripple's bake
pass already composes additively. The chord wave is that pass given a
direction, a wavelength and a window by the note.

**What it does.** Under Kessho the poly pressure of a held note (0xA0, the
swirl's dimension in Sumi) is the amplitude $A_k$ of a ripple-tine through
the note's drop:

$$
s_1' = s_1 + A_k\,\sin(\omega_k s_2 + \phi_k)\;w(s_2 - s_{2,k}),
$$

in the band's own coordinates $(s_1, s_2)$ along $(\hat e_1, \hat e_2)$. The
axis $\hat e_1$ is the pitch class's angle on the circle of fifths, fifteen
degrees a step so the twelve classes are twelve distinct orientations across
a half turn: C runs flat, G fifteen degrees up, D thirty, and round to F. The
wavenumber $\omega_k$ is the octave's: four periods across the canvas at
octave three, doubling every octave up, between two and thirty-two. The
window $w$ is a $C^2$ bump across the band, two and a half drop radii either
side of the note, so the tine spans the canvas along $\hat e_1$ and is
windowed only in $s_2$; the phase $\phi_k$ puts a crest on the note. The
pressure drives the amplitude as deltas, as the ripple bake does: pressing
in lays the wave, easing off takes it back, holding adds nothing, and the
wave stays baked when the note lifts.

**The lattice.** Voices compose as separate passes. One note is a band of
parallel ripples; two notes are two bands at their classes' angles, a
lattice where they cross; three and more the crossing region of three bands.
Each pass is a shear by a function of $s_2$ alone, windowed or not, so every
one has $\det J = 1$ exactly and so does the composition.

**Route (b), the rosettes.** The proposal's local rosettes — a
generating-function shear windowed in both coordinates, a two-by-two Newton
per note per texel — are the only way to the quasicrystalline three-, five-
and six-fold figures of the first draft; they are prototyped only after this
route has been seen on paper, the author's call.

## Ownership

* **Poly pressure** under Kessho, per note. No setting, no gesture of its own;
  the ripple's gesture serves.

## In the bench

`--chord-wave-test`: one note's band fitted to its sinusoid, the parallel
lines, nothing outside the band; a fifth's two bands and their crossing;
the renders in Sumi and Kessho. `--soak chord_wave` the four-part gate (the
pairs at $\pm A$); the headless suite the records.
