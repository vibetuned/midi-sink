midi-sink's founding rule bans Navier–Stokes *solvers*. Here Navier–Stokes
enters anyway — as an **exact solution**. The Lamb–Oseen vortex is the
closed-form decay of a 2-D line vortex in a viscous fluid, with tangential
velocity $v_\theta(r) = \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi r}\left(1 - e^{-r^2/r_c^2}\right)$.
Integrated over the pass interval it becomes a rotation angle:

$$
\theta(r) = \frac{\Gamma\,\Delta t}{2\pi r^2}\left(1 - e^{-r^2/r_c^2}\right),
\qquad \text{rotate } P \text{ about the centre by } -\theta(r).
$$

## Invariants

* **A pure rotation** — exactly area-preserving and, like the Rankine, an
  **exact map at any angle: no sub-stepping, ever**.
* **$C^\infty$.** Where the Rankine has its crease-ring kink at $R$, Lamb–Oseen
  is smooth everywhere: solid-body rotation in the core blending viscously
  into the same $1/r^2$ far field. The soft, organic twist beside the
  Rankine's mechanical one.
* **Numerical guard.** The expression is $0/0$-shaped at small $r$ and cancels
  in floating point; the shader evaluates it through `expm1`, and below
  $r \ll r_c$ uses the limit $\theta(0) = \Gamma \Delta t / (2\pi r_c^2)$
  directly.
* **Half-float freeze, accepted.** In RGBA16F, sub-quantum per-pass
  displacements near the exact centre round away — protective, never
  inflating. Field measurements at $r \to 0$ read low, not high; this is not a
  bug to chase.

## Ownership

* **Polyphonic key pressure (0xA0)** on the voice's note → swirl,
  unconditionally, in either pressure mode. No reference hardware sends it;
  the tablets' Play surface does — **pull the finger back** (Y down) and the
  note stirs.
* **The Marble-mode pressure gesture** (v0.6): a long press (Shift + right
  drag with a mouse) lays a drop; pull back and `sumi_add_vortex` with the
  Lamb–Oseen profile stirs around it, the drop as its core — the same pass
  the voice mapper emits, with the host supplying Γ·Δt and r_c.
* **Channel pressure (0xD0) with `press_mode = 1`** → swirl instead of the ink
  feed, so pressure-only hardware (ROLI, Osmose) can reach it. One consumer
  owns 0xD0.
* **The drop *is* the vortex core:** $r_c$ = the voice's current nominal
  boundary $R$, so its own rings rotate near-rigidly and stay coherent while
  the far field stirs the neighbours. $\Gamma$ per pass ∝ smoothed amount ×
  Δt (a delta rate, like every feed); the rotation sign defaults to the
  voice's band parity, so adjacent notes counter-rotate with zero
  configuration. Swirl passes fan out to every echo and count against the
  frame budget like any feed.

## Lineage

This is Aubrey Jaffer's own extension of his closed-form family: *The
Lamb–Oseen Vortex and Paint Marbling* (arXiv:1810.04646, 2018) derives the
displacement pattern of a decaying Lamb–Oseen vortex for marbling. midi-sink
arrived at the same operator independently, from the per-note pressure
dimension looking for a stir; finding his paper afterwards was the project's
happiest discovery. The vortex itself is in Lamb's *Hydrodynamics* — see the
[citations](../../reference/citations/).
