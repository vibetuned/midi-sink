The traditional waved comb displaces each line of the bath sideways by a sine
of its position. In the ripple frame (rotatable by `ripple_angle`):

$$
x_{src} = x - A\,\sin(k\,y + \varphi),\qquad y_{src} = y .
$$

## Invariants

* **A pure shear.** Each horizontal line translates rigidly; the Jacobian is 1
  identically at *any* amplitude, so the map never folds and is never
  sub-stepped.
* **A commutative group at fixed $(k, \varphi)$.** Applying $\Delta A$ per frame
  composes additively, so an LFO that breathes $A$ up and back to zero leaves
  the field **bitwise unchanged**. Changing $k$ or $\varphi$ between passes
  breaks commutativity and bakes residue in — and that residue *is* marbling,
  the waved-comb feathering. The boundary is deliberate and documented.
* **Two insertion points, one switch (`ripple_bake`).** *Live* (0): the ripple
  displaces the ink *sampling coordinate* at composite time — a view effect;
  nothing accumulates, the water shimmers and stills. *Bake* (1): a real
  deformation pass, delta-driven like the pinch. The washi grain never ripples
  (paper is screen-locked) and **the paper dip always samples the un-rippled
  field** — the print is what touches the water; the shimmer is surface motion.

## Ownership

* **Per-note pitch bend with `bend_mode = 1`.** The intent is subtle vibrato: a
  note's bend wobble shimmers the water instead of wiggling its drop. The drop
  *holds*; one consumer owns the note bend, and the switch is clean both ways
  (the glide target is untouched in mode 1, so switching back never emits a
  stale-delta tine). The master bend keeps its shear tine in both modes.
* **The amount is the bend's distance from centre:** $A = |\text{semitones}| / 6$,
  clamped — a ±0.5-semitone vibrato breathes about 8 %; last-writer-wins
  across voices, smoothed like every global control. So the water stills
  itself: bend re-centres → amount 0; the last note releases → amount 0; a
  1→0 mode flip zeroes the residual.
* **Bend-driven bake is permanent by design.** The emitted pass phase drifts
  with activity ($\varphi \mathrel{+}= |\Delta A| / A_{max} \cdot 1.5$ rad,
  wrapped), so an excursion never retraces exactly: each vibrato cycle lays a
  slightly shifted comb, a feathered record that accumulates like glide tines,
  while the dynamic still stills. CC-driven bake keeps $\varphi$ fixed — the
  pure composing-back property, preserved for the regression gate.
* **CC 102 / CC 103** — an external amount handle (sharing the slot,
  last-writer-wins) and the wavelength, a flavour control resting mid-range;
  the strip's assignable wheels and the desktop's settings window take them.
  On the tablets the *Ripple* toggle selects `bend_mode = 1` and
  `ripple_bake = 1` together; desktop keeps a manual live/bake override.
