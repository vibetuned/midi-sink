**Lineage.** The stepped leader of a lightning discharge and the Lichtenberg
figure: a channel that advances in steps, forks, and ionizes the path it
takes. In the engine's terms it answers a finding of the
[spark reading](../anod/#the-spark-reading-v30): that reading lights the
ridges of strain, and a strike's own strain is a plateau — the Anod strike's
shear is a stack of triangle waves whose slope is piecewise constant, so
$\sigma$ across the blob is a set of terraces, every texel a local maximum,
and non-maximum suppression thins nothing. *A reader can thin a ridge and
cannot invent one; line-like looks need line-like operators.* The leader
writes the line.

**What it does.** Under Anod with *Glow reading* on spark, the note-on
strikes a **stroke along a tree** from the strike point *beside* its blast
and streamers — the drop and the shear episode of the [spark](../spark/)
stay, the leader grows out of them (the author's viewing: without the
impact the bolt lost its weight); *Glow reading* plasma keeps 2.0's strike
bitwise.
The path is a biased random walk: steps of $\ell = 0.012$ canvas heights
(thirteen texels at 1080p), the heading jittered $\pm 30°$ about the
strike's pitch axis — the layout's glide direction at the key; off the
lattice, radial from the centre — a fork with probability $p_b = 0.15$ at
each step, $\pm 45°$ off, the branch's count half its parent's, three
generations deep; the trunk's count $N = 8 + 32\,v$ for the velocity
$v \in [0, 1]$, from eight steps to forty. The walk's generator is a 32-bit
congruential stream seeded by the strike's draw from the instance's
[seed](../../guide/replay/) — the same draw the plasma's blob phase takes —
so a replay is bit-identical, and the tree is integer arithmetic on every
platform; the stroke's draw comes first, the blob's phase draw after it.

**The shear.** Each segment is a slip along its own direction $\hat D$:

$$
P_{\text{src}} = P - a\,w(P)\,\hat D,\qquad
w = b\!\left(\tfrac{|t|}{h}\right)\, b_{\parallel}(s),\qquad
b(u) = 1 - u^3(10 - 15u + 6u^2),
$$

with $t$ the distance across the segment and $s$ the coordinate along it,
$h = 0.003$ canvas heights the window's half-width (three texels at
1080p), $b_\parallel$ equal to one over the segment and the same quintic
bump over ramps of $2\ell$ past both ends, and the slip
$a = h\,(0.6 + 1.4\,v)$ — two to six texels. Across the band the slip is a
pure shear, $\det J = 1$ whatever the profile, as the [tine's](../tine/);
along it the ramps compress or dilate by at most
$a\,b'_{\max}/\text{ramp} = 1.875/4$, so $\det J \ge 0.53$ for one
sub-step and the operator is **class sub-stepped**, under the same rule as
the [burst](../burst/) and the [wake](../wake/): the per-pass slip is at
most a quarter of the window's along-length, which at these constants is
one sub-step at every velocity. Every window is finite: nothing is pulled in
from off the sheet.

**The channel is charged.** The reading lights charge only, and a strike's
charge is a small disc; the stroke walks far beyond it, into water. So the
pass writes the strike's own ink band along the segment's core — within
$h$ of the path, round caps at the joints, the band's phase graded across
as a drop's is, the strike's aux (its hue) with it — while the slip above
stays: the leader *is* the ionized path, and the strain the slip leaves on
it is what the reading lights. The strain buffer's leader rides the tip
(strain being added over fifty milliseconds), the afterglow holds the
channel behind it in the strike's colour, and the bloom sees the leader
alone.

**The episode.** The stroke advances at 120 sub-steps a second — one a frame
at the scripted clock, a fractional credit at any other rate — every alive
branch together; a frame's sub-steps are composed in time order in **one
pass per stroke**, up to eight entries, so fifteen strokes are fifteen
passes a frame whatever their depth. If the frame budget refuses the pass,
the stroke waits a frame and loses nothing.

**Gesture route.** `sumi_add_leader(inst, x, y, dir_x, dir_y, energy, seed)`
for the stylus and the scripts: the drift along `(dir_x, dir_y)`, the reach
from `energy`, the tree from `seed`, a band of its own from the drop counter.

## Invariants

* **The class gate.** Under `--soak leader` the bare shear (the channel's
  charge off: a deposit, excluded by nature as every strike's drop is) reads
  a pre-image Jacobian of min 0.763 and mean 1.00000 everywhere after one
  sub-step; 500 pairs of a segment and its first-order negative leave 0.15
  texel of pre-image drift and hold the ink mass within 0.9 %; six thousand
  frames of strokes at gesture rate grow the mass by 3.4·10⁻⁵ a pass — the
  along-ramps' resampling, inside the class's rate bar — and the erosion
  reads below the glide tine's.
* **Deterministic.** The press scene with strokes twice from one seed is
  bitwise on the field; the plasma strikes' field differs — the strike's
  shape follows the mode.
* **A ridge, not a plateau.** One stroke at full velocity lights ridges 1.7
  texels wide at 1080p under the reading, read beyond its strike's blob and
  streamers; the blob episode under the same reading keeps a third of its
  lit texels with the suppression on, every one within two texels of the
  plasma silhouette — the suppression takes the lower terraces' edges and
  nothing more. The gate's red is this blob, reproduced on purpose.
* **Branches are geometry.** On the charge's own skeleton (the channel the
  stroke writes, thinned, its spurs pruned) six strokes alone at $p_b = 0.15$
  end in 3.2 tips each, 1.8 more than the same six at $p_b = 0$, which end
  in 1.3 — a single leader's two ends, less what the thinning takes. The
  strikes' torn charge under plasma has 0.09 endpoints per hundred texels.
* **The afterglow on a stroke** decays to $1/e$ in *Spark afterglow*
  (0.359 against 0.368, on a stroke alone through the gesture route; the
  gate found the strain pass comparing a float $\sigma$ against its
  half-rounded self, a permanent activity floor on strained texels, fixed
  at this step), and the leader at the tip is seven times the channel's
  crest in linear light with the bloom off (0.72 against 0.10) — the
  channel's gain 0.4, set here.
* **Velocity is reach.** The frames a stroke lives and the channel it
  charges beyond its strike's blob both rise with velocity: 22, 31, 47 and
  51 frames, 0.1, 2.5, 4.9 and 5.9 thousand texels at 32, 64, 96 and 127.
* **The pass budget.** A fifteen-voice storm of strokes at depth three costs
  one pass per stroke per frame of its life over the storm's total, beside
  the strikes' own drops and shear episodes, and walks out at frame 55.

**What stays open.** The walk was kept at the viewing and plasma stays
Anod's default reading, the stroke a setting away; dielectric breakdown (a
Laplacian solve per strike) stays a later alternative if the walk ever
reads as too regular, and the constants — $\ell$, $h$, $p_b$, the rate —
are the author's to retune.
