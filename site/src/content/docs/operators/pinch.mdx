Pinching the paper folds the water toward one axis and stretches it along the
other. In pinch-local coordinates (rotated by the fold angle about the pinch
centre):

$$
x_{src} = x\,e^{+k\,w(s)},\qquad y_{src} = y\,e^{-k\,w(s)},\qquad
s = x\,y,\quad w(s) = e^{-|s|/S} .
$$

A naive global saddle ($w \equiv 1$) acts on the whole canvas; a naive radial
window breaks incompressibility. Windowing by the **streamline value**
$s = xy$ does neither: $s$ is conserved along every hyperbolic trajectory of
the saddle, so the exponent is constant along any path and the map stays
closed-form with $\det = 1$ *exactly*.

## Invariants

* **Exactly area-preserving** — the Jacobian determinant is 1 everywhere, for
  any $k$.
* **Honest caveat, stated as a feature:** the window cannot decay along the two
  fold axes themselves ($s = 0$ there), so the pinch's arms run outward as
  fading creases — which is what pinched paper physically does.
* **$k$ is always a smoothed delta**, never an absolute controller value:
  absolute feeding integrates into runaway strain. Its sign swaps which axis
  compresses — a naturally bipolar gesture.
* **Two looks, one switch.** `pinch_variant` 0 is this Hamiltonian saddle;
  1 is **crossed tines** — two perpendicular, opposing tine passes through the
  pinch point. Softer, lumpier, directional; costs two passes per emission. It
  was the prototype rival kept after a pick-by-eye comparison, because the two
  are different instruments, not a better and a worse one.

## Ownership

* **CC 74 with `slide_mode = 1`:** smoothed slide deltas drive the pinch at the
  drop (pitch-axis fold) — the ROLI's vertical slide as a fold. With
  `slide_mode = 0` (default) CC 74 keeps its per-drop hue role; the choice is a
  params setting, never a silent rebinding.
* **The pen:** with `slide_mode = 1`, the stylus's CC 74 drives `sumi_add_pinch`
  at the pen position with the fold axis from the pen's **azimuth**; that
  CC 74 then goes outbound only, because a looped copy would pinch twice.
* **Two-finger pinch** (tablets, Marble mode): the fold axis is the
  finger-to-finger line, $k$ comes from the scale deltas.
* **Desktop:** Shift-drag — drag distance is the strength delta, drag angle the
  fold axis.

## In the scene

`k` is the per-pass strength (negative flips the axes), `θ` the fold angle,
`n` how many delta passes are stacked, `v` the variant.
