## The source

The sound engine has two sources and a third that is both: the **sampler**
(a Decent Sampler instrument, or a WAV, or the sine), **Suzu**, and
**layered** — every note strikes a sampled voice and a synth body in the
same voice. *Source* lives in the Sound section on the desktop and on the
tablets' Sound page; switching the source ends every voice. The sampler's
side is the [sound guide's](../../guide/sound/).

## The desktop: every knob of the current voice

The desktop's *Sound* section carries the whole synth. *Suzu voice* picks
the kind — *one cell*, *modal lattice*, *Verlet string*, *hybrid string*,
*Duffing cell*, *kicked rotor*, *flute (bore + jet)*, *saxophone (reed +
cone)*, *trumpet (lips + flare)* — and the section shows that kind's knobs
and no other's. They persist in the settings file as `suzu_*` and travel in
a [session preset](../../guide/presets/) as its `suzu` block.

| Voice | Knobs |
|---|---|
| every voice | *Suzu level*; *Suzu release (s)*, the declared T60; *Suzu cutoff (Hz)* (the filter at CC 74's centre; 20000 bypasses it) and *Suzu resonance*; *Suzu: the press blows* (channel pressure as the mouth of the bow and the winds) |
| one cell | *Suzu shear* and *Suzu shear kind* (cubic, triangle fold) |
| modal lattice | *Suzu preset* (harmonic string, stiff bar, bell, glass, plucked string), *Suzu modes* (up to 16), *Suzu coupling*, *Suzu decay (s)*, *Suzu brightness decay* (the highs' extra rate), *Suzu stiffness* (B), *Suzu pluck position*; the bow: *Suzu bow onset (s)*, *Suzu bow position* |
| Verlet string | *Suzu string nodes*, *Suzu string decay (s)*, *Suzu pickup*, *Suzu pluck position* |
| hybrid string | *Suzu bridge (Hz)*, *Suzu bridge modes*, *Suzu bridge coupling*, *Suzu bridge decay (s)*, *Suzu loop loss* (the one-zero's gain), *Suzu pickup* |
| Duffing cell | *Suzu Duffing beta*, *Suzu drive*, *Suzu drive ratio*, *Suzu decay (s)* |
| kicked rotor | *Suzu rotor K* (the rest K; the mod wheel sweeps it up to 2.5), *Suzu decay (s)* |
| flute | *Suzu bore cells*, *Suzu bore loss*, *Suzu bore corner (Hz)*, *Suzu bore wall (s)*; the jet: *gain*, *drive*, *delay (periods)*, *Q*, *noise*; *Suzu breath reference*, *Suzu breath range* |
| saxophone | the bore's knobs; the reed: *Suzu reed (Hz)*, *Q*, *opening*, *closing pressure*, *flow*, *noise*; *Suzu cone apex* (the truncation) |
| trumpet | the bore's knobs; the lips: *Suzu lip ratio*, *Q*, *opening*, *compliance*, *flow*, *lip range (octaves)* (CC 74's reach); *Suzu partial* (which peak the note sits on); *Suzu bell start*, *Suzu bell flare*, *Suzu brass* |
| the modulator | *Suzu modulator* (none, → cutoff, → coupling, → rotor K, → drive), *depth*, *rate* |
| the trace | the [orbit trace's](../orbit-trace/) section |

A patch the load gates refuse — a coupling the compensation cannot place,
a stiffness past the sampling bound, a junction that gains, a bore that
would leak energy — is refused with a note in the log and the live patch
stands.

## The patch table: eleven named patches

The sound engine owns one table of named patches, and every shell lists it:
the desktop's *Suzu patch* picker (with *Load patch*, which puts the patch
into the knobs — the knobs stay yours after), and the tablets' Sound page
picker, where the patch is the whole setting.

| Patch | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Bowed string** | the modal harmonic string under the breath bow — the one that sings under the brass layouts' valves and slide |
| **Bell**, **Flute**, **Saxophone**, **Trumpet** | the lattice's bell preset; the three winds at their defaults |
| **Dan Tranh (VCSL)**, **Glockenspiel (VCSL)**, **Tubular bells (VCSL)**, **Concert harp (VCSL)** | modal patches fitted to the Versilian Community Sample Library's recordings of those instruments — the partials' ratios, weights and decays measured from the samples |
| **Tenor sax (VCSL)**, **Baroque recorder (VCSL)** | the reed and the jet fitted the same way: the reed's threshold and closing pressure, the bore's loss and corner, the jet's noise |

The six fitted patches are the same numbers the [lab](../lab/) auditions
under its *VCSL* links and the same files in the tree's `presets/`; a test
pins the table to the files, so the three cannot drift. The Sound row names
what sounds — "Suzu: Tubular bells (VCSL)", or the sampler's instrument, or
both under the layered source — and the *Local Control* switch decides
whether the play surface sounds inside.

## The press, and the breath

Channel pressure is the output gain ($0.35 + 0.65\,p$) and the Duffing
drive's amplitude, and with *the press blows* it is also the mouth of the
bow and the winds — two consumers, flagged as such. Breath (CC 2, with CC 11
as the alias) is always the mouth. On a tablet the play surface's push-away
is the press, so the Bowed string and the winds speak under a finger's
pressure; a wind controller's breath plays them as it plays the ink feed.

## In a preset

A session preset's `suzu` block carries the source and every
`voxo_suzu_params_t` field by name; the sampler's instrument is not in it
(a library is a file of yours, never copied into a preset). The schema rule
holds: a preset from a version that knew fewer knobs loads with the
defaults for the rest.
