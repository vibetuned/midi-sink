## The Jaffer tribute

Aubrey Jaffer's three marbling animations on his
[MIT CSAIL pages](https://people.csail.mit.edu/jaffer/Marbling/) — *Bouquet*,
*Latte* and *Wave* — each open and close with a title card that credits their
music: three Turkish tunes popular for international folk dancing, published
as ABC notation on his own site. Read from the credit frames:

| Animation | Credited tune | Source named in the credits |
|---|---|---|
| *Bouquet* (rake patterns) | **Kendime** | `voluntocracy.org/Music/Kendime.abc` |
| *Latte* (a single stylus, in coffee) | **Ali Paşa** | `voluntocracy.org/Music/AliPasa.abc` |
| *Wave* (the Spanish wave) | **Rampi Rampi** | `voluntocracy.org/Music/RampiRampi.abc` |

**The piece: *Ali Paşa*** — the tune of *Latte*, the one animation Jaffer
made with a single stylus rather than a rake, which is what midi-sink's pen
does. Performed by the author on midi-sink: the instrument built on Jaffer's
equations, playing the music he set them to. Only the *composition's* status
matters for a performance by the author, and it was checked rather than
assumed: *Ali Paşa* (first line *Arpa ektim biçemedim*) is an anonymous
Turkish folk song — a lament for Ali Pasha of Van — held in the Turkish Folk
Music Archive (no. 398, collected by Muzaffer Sarısözen from the Van region)
and marked *Trad.* in Jaffer's own transcription; the dance to it was set by
Bora Özkök, but the dance is not what is performed here. A traditional,
anonymous composition of that age is in the public domain. The two alternates
are also traditional: *Rampi Rampi* (*Çadırımın Üstüne*, a 9/8 karşılama,
first recorded in 1946 and credited *Traditional* on every release) and
*Kendime*, a Turkish folk-dance tune presented to international dancers by
Özkök. The recording, when made, appears as the first card above.

## Turkish moiré

Jaffer's *Pigment Transport in Paint Marbling* analyses the Turkish moiré
pattern; a scripted moiré scene as a bridge between this gallery and the
[operator book](../operators/) is an optional addition noted for later and not
part of this release.
