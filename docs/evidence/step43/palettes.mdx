Every palette in midi-sink is the same small object: **stops** of linear RGB
along the depth axis — the ink's thickness in Sumi, the charge's glow in
Anod — a **depth curve** that says how the depth walks the ramp, a **hue
drift** by which each drop leans toward an **accent** colour of its own, and
the tone of the **clear water** between the rings. The composite reads
nothing else. The medium's three built-in palettes are that object too —
sumi black, indigo and ochre; electric blue, plasma orange and phosphor
green — two-stop palettes of one colour drifting toward their accent, and
they print bitwise as they did before the model existed.

**The identity guardrail.** A palette chooses hues. It does not touch the
washi's soak, the pooled ink's near-black, or the way strain lights a
charge: those are the medium's, and the same for every palette. The curated
library is the identity statement; the editor is freedom within it.

**The library.** Beside the built-ins, presets considerate of colour-blind
players: *Cobalt & amber* — the Okabe–Ito blue and orange, safe under
deuteranopia and protanopia — as ink and drift or as charge and halo, and
the perceptual ramps *Viridis* and *Cividis*, thin ink bright to pooled ink
dark, dim charge violet to burning charge yellow. Loading one fills the
custom slot as a starting point.

**The ring.** A palette-morph control travels the ring from the active
palette — built-in to built-in as always; from the custom slot, through the
three built-ins and back.

## Invariants

* **Bitwise.** The canonical script printed under every built-in of both
  media, at rest and under morph, hashes as it did through the old tables.
* **One path.** The composite has no palette table; a built-in, a preset
  and the custom slot are the same bytes read the same way.
* **Round trip.** Every preset set through the core comes back byte-equal.

## Ownership

* `sumi_palette_t` (2–8 stops, `depth_gamma`, `depth_floor`, `hue_drift`,
  `accent_rgb`, `clear_rgb`), `sumi_set_palette` / `sumi_get_palette`;
  `active_palette_id` 0–2 the built-ins, 3 the custom slot;
  `sumi_palette_preset_count` / `sumi_palette_preset` the library;
  `SUMI_CTL_PALETTE_MORPH` the ring.
