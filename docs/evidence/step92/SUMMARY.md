## Found on the way

* A scripted edit left `PARAM_ID`'s closing brace behind a trailing comment: the marble loaded nothing, the web gate read "no field dump within 60 s … WebGPU unavailable" with an empty console and the scene sweep retried every scene. `node --input-type=module --check` named the line. A web script is syntax-checked after every scripted edit now.
* The Toda ring's pitch is a spectral line, never a period: a period detector reads a beat on the linear ring (the modes are the lattice's sin(mπ/N)) and a sub-multiple on the train; the suite's measure is the lab's.
* The billiard's quantised bounce is a bigger share of the period as the note rises (1.45 % off at A3, 4.73 % at A5 for the naive reflection); the red reads A5.
* The Dirac network has no red of its own, and the strings' red does not reach it: the naive bow solve at the force 2.0 stays in bound on the network (the ledger closes to 1.5e-6, the gain under the work) — the bow acts through the chain's own kick scale. The page says so; its check gates the measured fact.
* The scene sweep's 25-s window left the first scenes of a cold Chrome unfinished on this Mac; the window is a parameter, 60 s by default.
* The lab wasm's export list had carried the strike exports of steps 79–80 only through the `used` attribute; they are listed now.
