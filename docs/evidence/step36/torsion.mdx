A vortex rotates every point about its centre by an angle that depends only
on the radius. The [exponential and Rankine profiles](../vortex/) make that
angle fall off monotonically; the wave torsion makes it **oscillate**:

$$
\theta(r) = A\,\sin(k\,r - \varphi)\,e^{-r/R},
\qquad \text{rotate } P \text{ about the centre by } -\theta(r).
$$

Concentric rings turn alternately one way and the other — a crest at
$k r - \varphi = \pi/2$, a trough half a wavelength further out — and the
whole pattern fades with the e-folding length $R$. Because $r' = r$, the map
is a rotation on every circle: **exactly area-preserving, exact at any
amplitude, no sub-stepping ever** — the vortex lemma verbatim. Advance
$\varphi$ over time and the rings travel outward at speed $\omega / k$: a
shockfront, a diffraction pattern, the discharge ring of the Anod medium.

## Invariants

* **Class: exact.** A rotation by $\theta(r)$ preserves $r$, so
  $\det J = 1$ everywhere and at any $A$; the $(+A, -A)$ pair inverts
  analytically. Measured on the desktop harness: two markers on a crest and a
  trough turn by $0.364$ and $-0.320$ rad against the analytic $0.376$ and
  $-0.332$, and return to within $3\cdot10^{-4}$ rad after the pair.
* **Oscillatory shear resamples rougher than smooth shear.** The pre-image of
  an exact torsion pair wanders 5.5 texels over a thousand passes where the
  smooth vortex profiles wander 1.5–2.7 — the medium's, not the operator's
  (the markers come home). Recorded in the conservation gate's bar.
* **The medium gains a little mass under it.** At the default wavelength a
  6000-pass stream grows ink mass by 2.3%, all of it interior at the ring
  boundaries, an order of magnitude below edge-clamp duplication and the
  reason the gate's fabrication criterion is a rate (`DECISIONS_5 #21`).

## Ownership

* **A third vortex profile**, `SUMI_VORTEX_TORSION`, reachable through every
  vortex route: the Marble-mode right-drag, the mod wheel and the Airwave's
  left hand with the profile set to Torsion, and `sumi_add_vortex`.
* **$k$ and $\varphi$ are flavour controls** — `SUMI_CTL_TORSION_K` (rests
  mid-range, $2\pi\cdot4$ to $2\pi\cdot40$ per canvas height) and
  `SUMI_CTL_TORSION_PHASE` ($0$ to $2\pi$), unmapped in the core like the
  ripple's wavelength; the desktop maps CC 104 and 105 to them. Under the
  Anod medium's binding table the per-note bend will drive $k$ (step 42).
* **The note-on sweep** (`torsion_sweep = 1`, off by default until the Anod
  tables own the strike): every strike also fires an outward wave of torsion
  around its drop — $\varphi$ advancing at $2\pi\cdot1.5$ rad/s, the
  amplitude decaying with $\tau = 0.6$ s, reach three strike radii — emitted
  as **per-frame rotation increments, never a total**, so two strikes in the
  same place add up and a frame the budget merges carries its increment over.
  The episode runs on its own clock: releasing the note does not cut it. It
  is the engine's first time-driven episode; the burst's age envelope and the
  spark's decay reuse the pattern.

## Lineage

Jaffer's vortex lemma — a rotation by any function of the radius is exactly
area-preserving — is the whole proof; the torsion only chooses an oscillating
function. The look is the electric medium's (MEDIUM §2.1): concentric
diffraction rings and travelling shockfronts, drawn with the same operator
the sumi medium stirs its water with.
