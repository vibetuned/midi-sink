## The feel change this carries (accepted, DECISIONS_3 #57)
cell_radius is also the joystick travel bound, and hostmpe's absolute
deadband floor (#16, 0.006 canvas-height) bites a smaller knob harder:
  natural     R=0.05714  deadband 10.5% of R  usable travel 0.05114
  accidental  R=0.03429  deadband 17.5% of R  usable travel 0.02828
So an accidental's usable travel is 55.3% of a natural's — a 45% cut,
not the 40% the radius alone suggests. Governs pressure, the 0xA0 swirl
half-axis and stylus CC74; bend is unaffected (identity beyond the
circle, #10).
