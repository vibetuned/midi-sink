# LIVE S-Pen legato traces on device (tools/pen_trace.py), all three
# playable lattices. The analyzer reconstructs sounding pitch
# (note + bend/171 at the MCM-declared ±48) per stroke from the byte
# log's stylus messages (src 4). The crossing seam is bounded LOCALLY:
# 2.5x the larger of (the ordinary per-event step just before that
# crossing, the stroke's median ordinary step) — inputs chosen so a
# defect cannot widen its own tolerance (an injected 50-cent jump at a
# crossing FAILS at 3.81x; the real traces sit at 0.43-0.46x).
