What's new in 2.0

• Sound inside: a sampler for Decent Sampler libraries (a zither plays at first launch) and Suzu, a built-in synth with eleven patches
• Anod, a second medium: the water read as an electric discharge
• Five new layouts: trumpet and trombone with valves and slide, Wicki–Hayden, strings, theremin
• Palettes, presets, and a prints ledger that re-exports at any size
• Record a session and replay it on any device
• A MIDI network session over Wi-Fi
