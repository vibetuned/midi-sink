First release.

midi-sink turns a MIDI instrument into ink on water. Every note is a drop: pressure feeds it, bends comb it, a stylus threads it.

• Marble mode — MPE controllers, wind controllers and plain keyboards paint the tray; fingers and the S-Pen play it too
• Play mode — a 15-voice MPE surface, out over USB-MIDI, Bluetooth LE and to on-device apps
• Eight pitch layouts, three ink palettes, a full CC map
• Save a print to your gallery

Free software. Collects nothing.
