# SYNTH SPECIFICATION: Symplectic Phase-Space Synthesis
**Companions: `SOUND_SPEC.md` (Voxo — this lives inside it), `MEDIUM_SPEC.md` (the shared philosophy), `PROJECT_SPEC.md`. Iteration expected — open points are marked `[ITERATE]`.**

**The thesis, stated once:** the visual engine composes det J = 1 maps on a canvas; this synth composes det J = 1 maps on **phase space** — each oscillator a 2D cell (x, y) where area preservation means *amplitude stability by construction* instead of *ink incompressibility*. Same mathematics, one octave down. Dissipation exists (filters resonate, notes decay) but is **declared, never accidental** — the audio analog of the operator class table. Working name `[ITERATE: Suzu (鈴, small bell — fits the modal voice); alternatives welcome]`; architecturally it is a **source module inside Voxo** (same callback, same normalizer-fed voice model, same mixer/bus effects — a patch beside a preset, never a second audio stack).

---

## 1. The DSP class table (every element declares one)

| Class | det of the per-sample map | Members | Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Symplectic** | = 1 exactly | magic-circle rotation, phase-space shears (waveshaping), mode-coupling shears, the kicked rotor | amplitude/energy confined by construction; the long-run drift test (§5) is the gate |
| **Dissipative (conformal)** | < 1, declared, uniform | SVF damping q, per-mode decay, release, the string's per-node damping | conformal contraction — phase-space shrinks by a declared constant factor; every decay traces to a coefficient; the energy-ledger test proves nothing else leaks |
| **Drive** | injection, not a map | strike kicks, press drive, filter input | bounded per sample; drive into an undamped cell must be a deliberate patch choice, flagged in the editor |
| **Self-excited** | ⋛ 1, state-dependent, bounded by construction | the breath bow (§2.6: energy-servo van der Pol) | negative damping ONLY below a target energy, positive above — a limit cycle, never free growth; gated by §5's limit-cycle test |
| **Lossless transport** | = 1 (measure-preserving delay) | the hybrid string's delay line / waveguide (§2.9) | energy-neutral by construction; every coupling INTO a transport must be passive — gated by §5's loop-passivity test |

Unconditional anti-dissipation (det > 1 regardless of state) remains banned — the audio equivalent of ink fabrication. The self-excited class is admitted precisely because its energy injection is servo-bounded: the map that gives can only give until the orbit reaches its target, then it takes.

## 2. The primitive cells

### 2.1 Magic circle oscillator (the rotation)
Per sample: `x −= ε·y; y += ε·x` (second shear uses the UPDATED x — the same evaluated-at-the-displaced-value subtlety the Chladni pass guards with a negative test; a "simultaneous" version spirals). Two unit-determinant shears; exact frequency ε = 2·sin(π·f/fs) — use the exact form, not the small-angle ε ≈ ω approximation, so tuning is cents-true across the keyboard. Quadrature for free: x and y sit ~90° apart — **every oscillator is a stereo/quadrature pair at zero cost** (stereo width, and single-sideband-style effects later).

**The MPE correction (load-bearing — this instrument BENDS):** retuning the plain magic circle mid-orbit changes the orbit's eccentricity — a ±48-semitone glide audibly amplitude-modulates. Ship the **Gordon–Smith form** (the equal-amplitude variant, one extra multiply) or renormalize state on retune `[ITERATE: Gordon–Smith default; measure both under the §5 sweep test]`. Without this, the synth's signature gesture (the glide) is its worst artifact.

### 2.2 Chamberlin SVF (the rotation, damped and driven)
`low += f·band; high = in − low − q·band; band += f·high` — the (low, band) core is the magic circle; q is the declared dissipation; the input is drive. Exact tuning f = 2·sin(π·fc/fs). **Honest stability ceiling:** the classic form misbehaves as fc approaches fs/6 at high resonance — v1 runs the filter stage at **2× oversampling** (which also serves §2.4), with the trapezoidal/zero-delay-feedback SVF named as the pragmatic escape hatch if the ceiling still binds `[ITERATE: Chamberlin@2x vs trapezoidal — the thematic choice vs the numerical one; decide by the §5 tuning test, not by ideology]`. Per-voice; CC74/slide → cutoff, resonance a patch knob (dissipation you can hear).

### 2.3 The kicked rotor (the signature voice — Chirikov, audible)
A magic circle kicked once per cycle with strength K: **the standard map as an oscillator.** Below the stochastic threshold: pure tone with orderly sidebands (FM-adjacent shimmer). Approaching K_c ≈ 0.9716: island chains — inharmonic partial clusters. Past it: deterministic chaos — pitched noise that still remembers its fundamental. **Mod wheel or pressure → K, delta-smoothed:** one knob sweeps order → shimmer → storm, the *sonic twin of the visual Chirikov step*, and the two can run from the same controller simultaneously — the water tears as the tone does. Chaos is by-design broadband: band-limit the kick and oversample this cell (§2.4); the noise floor is musical, aliasing is not.

### 2.4 Phase-space shears (waveshaping with a conscience)
`x′ = x + g(y)` distorts the orbit — harmonics from a det = 1 map: the tine, audible. Any g is legal (the spark-shear lesson: smoothness was never required — tri/fold shears give hard timbres), but **nonlinear stages alias**: they run inside the 2× oversampled section, and the fold shears get ×4 `[ITERATE: budget]`. Denormal warning, project-wide: decaying orbits glide into subnormal floats and the callback's cost explodes — **flush-to-zero/DAZ set on the audio thread at init** is a hard requirement, tested (§5).

### 2.5 Modal coupling (the voice as a lattice of cells)
A voice = N magic-circle modes (N ≈ 4–16 `[ITERATE]`) with per-mode tuning ratios and declared decays; **coupling = weak symplectic shears between cells** (cell A's x kicks cell B's y and back, alternating — the composition stays det = 1), so energy *migrates between partials without global growth*: a stable modal network with no energy bookkeeping, because the bookkeeping is the map. Ratio presets make the instruments: harmonic string, stiff bar, bell (inharmonic), glass — and **the plucked-string preset IS Karplus–Strong, modal form**: f_k = k·f₀·√(1+Bk²) (B = stiffness inharmonicity, 0 for nylon), conformal damping γ_k = α + βk² (highs die first, the KS decay law), and — answering the strike-position `[ITERATE]` in §3 — **pluck position p sets the modal kick profile in closed form: weight_k ∝ sin(kπp)/k²** (the triangular pluck's spectrum). Pluck near the bridge and the kicks brighten, exactly as on wood. **The swirl dimension (0xA0) → coupling strength:** the same gesture that stirs ink stirs the overtones — pull down and hear energy slosh between partials `[ITERATE: the thematically perfect binding; sign off by ear]`.

### 2.6 The breath bow (self-excited class — the wind player's reason to be here)
A magic-circle cell whose declared damping is **signed and state-dependent**: d = μ·(E_target − E), with E = x² + y² the cell's phase-space energy — the van der Pol structure with an explicit energy servo. Below the target orbit the cell is anti-damped (the bow grips); above it, damped (the bow slips): the composite is a **bounded limit cycle by construction**, never free growth. **Breath (CC2/aliases, the INK_FLOW dimension) → E_target; μ sets onset speed** (slow μ = a tone that blooms, fast = speaks instantly) `[ITERATE: μ from breath attack rate?]`. Zero breath ⇒ E_target = 0 ⇒ pure dissipation — **silence with no input is a gated property, not a hope**. Applied per-mode across the modal lattice with a bow-position profile (which partials the bow feeds) `[ITERATE: profile from CC or fixed per patch]`. This is the Brisa/Travel Sax payoff: sustained singing tones from the instruments that breathe — wind mode finally has a voice that holds. Physical stick-slip (Helmholtz-motion) bowing stays deferred; the servo bow is the honest v1 whose energy story is provable.

### 2.7 The orbit trace (the synth draws itself — cross-medium, no new math)
Each voice's cell traces an orbit in (x, y); the trace makes it VISIBLE by two routes, both built from existing parts:
* **Gesture route (the showpiece):** per video frame, decimate the audio-rate orbit to a short polyline (curvature-weighted, ≤ a per-voice segment budget `[ITERATE: 4–8 segments]`) and emit it as tine/wake segments at the voice's canvas position, scaled by orbit amplitude × a trace-scale param. **Class by inheritance** (the strictest-member rule — wake segments gate as sub-stepped); counts against the deform budget like any feed; per-voice toggle in the binding tables `[ITERATE: default ON for the kicked rotor in Anod — the chaos voice scribbling its own noise into the water is the demo]`. A pure sine stirs a small circle; a shear-distorted orbit combs its harmonics into the ink; the rotor past K_c scribbles. Timbre becomes ink through the actual state trajectory, not a proxy.
* **Scope route (the cheap sibling):** a live composite overlay drawing the orbit curves screen-space at the voice positions — non-destructive like the live ripple, excluded from the dip like the live ripple. Ships first; the gesture route is the one that marbles.

### 2.8 The Verlet string (direct FDTD chain)
A 1D mass–spring chain, M = 30–80 nodes, fixed ends, per sample and per interior node: `accel = (u[i+1] − 2u[i] + u[i−1])·k_spring; v[i] = v[i]·damping + accel·dt; u[i] += v[i]·dt` (symplectic Euler with conformal damping — all accelerations read the pre-update u). Pluck = triangular u-profile released from rest; audio = a pickup node (≈ M/4; a second pickup is free stereo `[ITERATE]`). **The CFL gate, non-negotiable:** the explicit scheme is stable only for **k_spring·dt² ≤ 1** (the 1–2–1 Laplacian's eigenvalue bound) — tuning couples M, k_spring and dt, so the constraint is enforced at patch-load with the proven-red control in §5, never trusted to taste. Where the modal preset (§2.5) gives the *spectrum* of a string, the chain gives its *body*: pickup position, re-plucking a still-ringing string, and buzzy nonlinear extensions later `[ITERATE: fret-buzz collision as a declared event]`. Cost is honest: M nodes × rate × voices, SIMD across nodes.

### 2.9 The hybrid string (transport + bridge)
The high-yield architecture: a classic KS **lossless delay line** (transport class) carries the traveling wave; its output drives a 2–3-cell **bridge** (magic-circle modes, optionally one Duffing cell for bridge nonlinearity); the bridge's *velocity* feeds back into the line. Body resonance and string–body coupling without a soundboard model — and without manufactured energy, because the coupling is **gated passive**: with every declared damping zeroed, the closed loop must sustain without growth (§5). The KS averaging filter is a declared conformal loss, not folklore.

### 2.10 Duffing cell & chaotic modulators
**Duffing = rotation + cubic phase-space shear** — §2.1 composed with §2.4's g(y) = −βy³·dt kick: already in the vocabulary, stable under the symplectic update across large swings. The musical point is the amplitude-dependent pitch: a hardening-spring bell **clangs sharp and settles flat as it decays** — a gesture no static wavetable owns. Driven, it is the second chaos voice beside the rotor (§2.3); it inherits §2.4's oversampling. **Chaotic modulators run at control rate, not audio:** a leapfrog double pendulum at ~1 kHz `[ITERATE: rate]` is the organic-drift source — bounded (Hamiltonian, energy set at trigger), aliasing-free by construction, routed to any smoothed parameter as a per-patch mod source `[ITERATE: how many, and does the CC map grow a mod-matrix corner?]`.

## 3. Voice architecture & MPE mapping (through the normalizer, as ever)
Strike → kick injection across modes via a strike-position profile (velocity → total kick, position `[ITERATE: from the layout's cell? a struck bar]` → modal weighting). Press → sustained small drive (bounded, into damped modes only per §1); **breath → the bow's E_target (§2.6)** — in wind mode the bow IS the press consumer. Glide → per-voice ε retune (Gordon–Smith). Slide/CC74 → SVF cutoff. Swirl → mode coupling. Lift → release ramps the declared decays. All under the existing one-consumer rules; the binding table is the author's to sign by ear — this time there is sound.

## 4. Engineering constraints (Voxo's contract, inherited)
Per-sample recursions in the callback: no locks, no allocations, no logging; SIMD across modes then voices `[ITERATE: structure-of-arrays layout]`; parameter changes smoothed at block edges (ε and q ramp per-sample across the block — a stepped ε is a click); FTZ/DAZ at thread init; the synth and the sampler share the mixer, the bus reverb/delay, master gain, and local-control.

## 5. Tests (DONE-style, the audible gates)
* **Drift test (the symplectic promise):** an undriven, undamped magic circle at A = 0.5 for 10 minutes: peak amplitude drift < 0.1 dB, frequency drift < 0.5 cent. The negative control (the naive simultaneous update) visibly spirals — proven red.
* **Glide test (the MPE promise):** a scripted ±48-semitone sweep over 2 s: amplitude ripple < 0.5 dB (Gordon–Smith on); the plain form's ripple printed beside it, not gated — the justification travels with the decision.
* **Energy ledger:** a bell patch struck once with all decays set to zero sustains ≥ 10 minutes within the drift bound (nothing undeclared leaks); with decays restored, the measured T60s match the declared coefficients within 5%.
* **Tuning:** SVF resonant peak and oscillator pitch within 2 cents across MIDI 21–108 at 44.1 k and 48 k.
* **Denormal gate:** a decaying-tail stress (64 voices released simultaneously) holds callback time flat to the end — no subnormal cliff.
* **The bow's limit cycle (the self-excited gate):** from silence and from 2× target amplitude alike, the cell converges to the breath-set orbit within a declared time constant; **zero breath → decay to silence** (no self-oscillation without input, proven, not hoped); at steady state the injected and extracted energy per cycle balance within 1% (the servo's ledger). The negative control — the servo with its extraction clamped off — grows without bound and is proven red.
* **The orbit trace's budget:** 10 voices with traces on hold the frame budget (segments merged per the echo rules, never starving other feeds); trace OFF is bit-identical to no-trace (the toggle is clean).
* **The CFL gate (the chain's ceiling):** patch-load rejects k_spring·dt² > 1 with a clear message; the proven-red control (bound exceeded by 5%) blows up within a second in the harness, archived. A tuning sweep across MIDI 21–108 respects the bound by re-deriving M/k_spring, pitch within 2 cents where representable.
* **Loop passivity (the hybrid's ledger):** all declared damping zeroed → the delay+bridge loop sustains ≥ 10 minutes within the drift bound (no manufactured energy); the negative control (coupling gain 1.05× the passive bound) grows and is proven red.
* **The Duffing chart:** a single struck note's pitch-vs-time curve archived (the clang-and-settle), plus the driven cell's order→chaos spectrogram beside the rotor's.
* **The rotor's chart:** K swept 0 → 2.5, spectrogram archived as evidence — the KAM transition, audible, beside its visual sibling in the docs.
